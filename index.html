👋 What this is

TMade Alex is a futures trader, a live streamer, the host of Meet the Trader on FundedNext, and a fashion photographer in New York. On 23 November 2026 a member of his community set up a home for him on GitHub, as a gift: his own website, tmadealex.github.io, and a few quiet rooms around it.

This AstroLog keeps him up to date on everything done to that home since. Each entry is one change, or one day’s changes: what changed, why, and where to see it. It is written for TMade Alex to read, in plain words, with no jargon left unexplained. Nothing here asks anything of him. If he would like anything changed or taken down, it is.

Nothing on these pages is financial advice. Search engines are asked not to list them until TMade Alex says otherwise.

🗂️ What’s here

  • Changelog: one entry per change, newest first. Each opens with a one-line summary, with the full story folded underneath.
  • Library: longer write-ups on how these pages work.
  • Ledger: every public change to his home’s code on GitHub, one line each, refreshed every day.
  • Roadmap and Sustainability: what could come next (his call), and what keeps these pages running.

✍️ Adding an entry

For whoever keeps this record. A new file in changelog/, named YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md:

---
title: "Plain-words title"
date: "YYYY-MM-DD"
priority: "P2-medium" # P1-high, P2-medium (the default) or P3-low
repo: "site" # site (his website) or github (his home on GitHub)
who: "A member of his community, with Alfred" # see PEOPLE in astro/src/data/site.ts
---

Open with a one-line TL;DR, then the full story inside a <details> block: what changed, why, and where to see it. Only what the commits say happened. His words and figures only where his own site already shows them, with their source. “TMade Alex” in full, always. Drafts wait in changelog/drafts/, which is never built; move a finished one up into changelog/ with git mv.

💻 Local development

sh scripts/install-hooks.sh   # once per clone
cd astro
npm ci
npm run dev

Only the built pages leave this repository: .github/workflows/sync-public.yml builds the site, runs the public-lane tripwire on what was built, and copies it to the public twin. PUBLIC-ALLOWLIST.txt lists that one path.
