The red pill and the blue pill on his trading desk

TL;DR: His trading desk gains the red pill and the blue pill: blue keeps his cyan; red shows the market's own green and red candles. The desk rises again over a terminal that boots, a rain of Pine Script and little candles falls behind the title, and the phone menu now only slides in from the right.
Read the full story

💊 What changed

  • The red pill and the blue pill on the desk. Blue keeps his cyan. Red shows the market’s own green and red candles (filled), an amber average line and a terminal-green rain, with a half-second glitch as it switches (none for anyone who asks for less motion). The choice is kept like the look, with no flash, in both languages.
  • The desk rises again from its base (tipped, smaller and lower, straightening as it scrolls up). In the space above it, a terminal boots: “Pulling up to the desk” with a blinking cursor, three lines typing in, and a bar filling with the climb, until the screen covers them.
  • The rain behind the title spells generic Pine Script down its columns (the kind of code charting tools run), with little candles falling between. Nothing in it is a strategy of his.
  • The phone menu waits just off the right edge and slides in from there. Escape closes it, the keyboard stays inside it while it is open and returns to the menu button after, and opening a new page closes it.

💡 Why

  • The desk had lost its rise. A fix the day before took away the gap above the desk by tipping it less, and the moment of the screen rising into place went with it. The rise is back, and the space it leaves now does something: it shows the desk switching on as you pull up to it.
  • A nod to the red pill and the blue pill of The Matrix. The desk now lets a visitor choose: the blue pill keeps his own colours; the red pill shows the market’s. It is a toy, and it starts there: more can follow.
  • The phone menu still snapped in from the left on some phones, though it sits on the right. It no longer uses the browser’s own pop-up window, which some phones drew from the left for a moment; it waits just off the right edge and can only slide in from there.

👀 Where to see it

The trading page, and the phone menu on any page.

Pasted into a message, it arrives with its own preview card.
