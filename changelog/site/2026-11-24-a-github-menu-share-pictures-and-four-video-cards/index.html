A GitHub menu, a picture for every shared link, and the four seats as video cards

TL;DR: Three changes after the launch. The menu has a GitHub entry with his home and the site's source, and the phone menu is solid enough to read over any page. Every page now has its own picture and sentence when its link is shared. And the four seats on the front page open his videos at the moment he says it, with FundedNext and TradersPost named, YouTube's own thumbnails, and the privacy wording updated to say so.
Read the full story

🧭 A GitHub menu, and a solid phone menu

  • The “Source” pill is now a GitHub menu, built like Explore: TMadeAlex on GitHub (his home) and Source on GitHub (the site’s own code). The status bar and the note at the end of each page carry both links.
  • The phone menu and the dropdowns were too see-through. They are now solid enough to read over any page.

🖼️ A picture for every shared link

When a link to his site is pasted into a message or a post, it now arrives with a picture and a sentence (1200 by 630 pixels, one per page) in his TRADERMADE colours: his name with the cyan dot, his roles, made-up candles, viewfinder corners, and a lens for photography. No photograph of his is drawn in; his pictures stay on his own site. The pictures are drawn by a script from the site’s own words, colours and fonts, with nothing fetched. Phones and apps that want a picture for the icon get the ring and the dot.

▶️ The four seats, as video cards

  • Each of the four seats on the front page is now one link to its video, at the moment he says the words quoted, showing YouTube’s own thumbnail (from YouTube, not copied here), a breathing cyan play button, the channel it is on, and the time. Under each picture, the seat’s moving drawing stays: candles, sound levels, a humming room, a viewfinder that hunts for focus.
  • The host seat and the trading page now say that Meet the Trader is on FundedNext’s YouTube channel, and the ALPHAS interview is named as TradersPost’s. “Teacher” joins his turning roles and the streamer seat, from his own word “educational”.
  • The four cards are evenly filled, and each role fits on one line.

🔒 The privacy wording, updated

The footer, the colophon and the README now say plainly what a visit asks for: this site, his photography site for his photographs, and YouTube for the four video pictures, and nothing else. Those two picture servers see a visit, as any picture server does. The site sends nothing about anyone.

💡 Why

So a link shared with TMade Alex, or by him, looks like his from the first glance; so a visitor goes straight to the moment in each video; and so the privacy note stays true now that four pictures come from YouTube.

👀 Where to see it

The front page (the four seats), and the GitHub menu at the top of every page.

Pasted into a message, it arrives with its own preview card.
