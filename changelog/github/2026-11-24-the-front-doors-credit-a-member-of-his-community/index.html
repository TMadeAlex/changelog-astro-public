The front doors credit a member of his community, so the light stays on TMade Alex

TL;DR: The credit lines on his home's front doors (the organisation page, his site's gift line, and the README of each public repository) now say "a member of his community" set it up as a gift, without a name. Not to hide anything: out of respect, so the credit takes none of his light.
Read the full story

🤝 What changed

  • His site (23 November): the gift line, the holding page, the contact page’s note and the risk note say “a member of his community”. The person who set the site up is not named.
  • The organisation page (23 November): the gift note, the people row and the footer stop naming the person who set the home up.
  • The front-door READMEs (24 November) of the organisation page, his site, the brand shelf, and the public copies of the reading room and the procedures now credit “a member of his community”.

Practical lines elsewhere (who holds a key, whom to message, the licences) are unchanged, so nothing that someone might need to find is hidden.

💡 Why

The home is his. A name on every front door would take some of the attention that belongs to TMade Alex.

👀 Where to see it

The line near the top of his site, and the front page of his home on GitHub.

Pasted into a message, it arrives with its own preview card.
