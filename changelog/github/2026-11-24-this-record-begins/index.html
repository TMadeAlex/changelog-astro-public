This record begins: an AstroLog for his home

TL;DR: His home now has an AstroLog: this plain-words record of everything done to it since it was set up, newest first, so TMade Alex can see what changed, why, and where, without opening GitHub. It wears his two looks, carries its own fonts and icons, sets no cookies, and asks search engines not to list it.
Read the full story

📜 What changed

Two repositories joined his home:

  • changelog-astro (private): where each entry is written.
  • changelog-astro-public (public): the finished pages you’re reading, at tmadealex.github.io/changelog-astro-public.

The record has a changelog (one entry per change, with the story folded underneath), a library (how things work), a ledger (every public change to his home’s code, one line each, refreshed every day), a map of everywhere these pages lead, and roadmap and sustainability pages. Press / anywhere to search it all, including the pages of his own site.

It wears the same two looks as his site, Night and Morning, and remembers the one a visitor already chose there. It speaks Spanish in its menus (the entries stay in English for now, and it says so). A few made-up candles drift behind each page, and PARTY_MODE is one of its motion choices.

Only the finished pages are ever published, after a machine reads every one of them; the library has how that works. Giving, a pledge and any wallet are switched off, there are no analytics, and no one’s email is used for a picture.

💡 Why

So TMade Alex can keep up with everything done to his home since it was shared with him, in one calm place, in plain words.

👀 Where to see it

The changelog, the ledger and the map.

Pasted into a message, it arrives with its own preview card.
