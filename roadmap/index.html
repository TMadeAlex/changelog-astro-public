🧭 Roadmap

TL;DR: Ideas, never asks, and no dates. Some things wait on TMade Alex's word: whether he wants the home at all, his own art on its shelf, the Spanish read by someone who speaks it as their own language, and whether search engines may list his pages. Everything here is his call.

What could come next for TMade Alex’s home on GitHub. What’s already done is in the changelog, and what keeps it all running is on the sustainability page. This is also the repository’s ROADMAP.md.

⏳ Waiting on TMade Alex’s word

  • Whether he wants it. The home is a gift, set up before he had answered. If he would rather not have it, any part of it can go private or come down the same day.
  • His TRADERMADE art on the brand shelf (brand-assets). It waits on a branch on one computer, unpushed, until he says yes.
  • The Spanish. The site’s Spanish pages, and the Spanish menus here, are a helper’s translation. They say so until he, or someone he trusts who speaks Spanish as their own language, has read them.
  • Being found. His site and these pages ask search engines not to list them. That changes only when he says so.
  • Support from a wallet. His site’s Support window has a part for a crypto address that only he can give. While it is empty, nothing is shown, and no helper ever supplies one.

💡 Ideas, if he wants them

Each is written up in his private proposals room, as an offer:

  • A TMade Discord helper that answers “when is the next stream?” in the words of his own public pages.
  • Linear, if he would like help to keep going.

🤝 Who holds the keys

Today the member of his community who set the home up holds the organisation and the key that publishes these pages. The plan is that TMade Alex holds them himself, as an owner of his own organisation, once he has accepted the invitation. Details on the sustainability page.
